WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. Free Climb (new mode): clear the mountain once in Regular mode, then a button on the victory
   screen and in the main menu opens the mountain as a sandbox. A map of the
   whole climb sits in the top-right corner (click or M to expand), right-click
   on it teleports the player to the nearest ledge. Achievements are disabled in
   this mode, nothing is saved, and the summit air wall is off so you can jump
   off. Also fixes: leaving the summit restores normal ice friction.
2. Profiles (new accounts): each profile has its own 5 save slots, achievements,
   cleared status and Free Climb unlock, in
   %APPDATA%\A Slipery Climb\profiles\<id>\. Existing loose save/achievement
   files are migrated into the first profile automatically. settings.json stays
   global.
3. Run timer: shown next to the Exit button, pauses while the Free Climb map is
   open, stops at the summit, shown on the victory screen, stored in saves.
4. Quit confirmation: menu Exit and the window close button ask first.
5. Fixes: Esc returns to the menu from Saves / Settings / Profiles; all buttons
   are blue again (in-game Exit, sliders, fullscreen toggle, language picker,
   Free Climb buttons); "Back"/"Return" unified to "Back".
6. Version bumps: version_info.txt and the macOS Info.plist in the workflow.
