A SLIPERY CLIMB

A small, slippery climbing game about a Santa who has to get up a very big
mountain to deliver presents.

There is no walk button. You move by throwing candy and letting the explosions
push you around: throw backward to slide forward, blast-jump over gaps, and ride
moving platforms up the mountain.

- Hand-made level (Getting Over It style): stairs, steep ladders, drop jumps,
  ferries, elevators and curved moving platforms
- Candy-bomb movement: throw, blast, slide, climb
- 9 languages: English, 中文, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Deutsch, Français,
  Italiano, Español
- Intro and victory cutscenes (skippable), idle chatter, leveled achievements
- 5 save slots; clearing the mountain deletes that slot, so every run is fresh

Controls
  Mouse        aim and throw candy
  Esc          save and return to the menu
  Space/Enter  skip cutscene

Windows build, no installation needed. Settings and saves are stored in
%APPDATA%\A Slipery Climb.
