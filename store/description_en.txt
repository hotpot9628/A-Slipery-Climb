A SLIPERY CLIMB

A small, slippery climbing game about a Santa who has to get up a very big
mountain to deliver presents.

There is no walk button. You move by throwing candy and letting the explosions
push you around: throw backward to slide forward, blast-jump over gaps, and ride
moving platforms up the mountain.

- Hand-made level (Getting Over It style): stairs, steep ladders, drop jumps,
  ferries, elevators and curved moving platforms
- Candy-bomb movement: throw, blast, slide, climb
- Easy mode with 8 checkpoints (picked per new save) or the classic normal mode
- Free Climb: clear the mountain once in Regular mode, then explore it as a
  sandbox — a map of
  the whole climb, right-click to teleport anywhere, achievements off
- Profiles (accounts): several players on one computer, each with their own
  save slots, achievements and "cleared" status
- Run timer next to the Exit button, frozen on the victory screen
- Full voice-over in 9 languages for idle chatter and fall taunts, with separate
  sound, music and voice volume sliders
- Fullscreen or windowed display, menus scale to the window
- 9 languages: English, 中文, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Deutsch, Français,
  Italiano, Español
- Intro and victory cutscenes (skippable), idle chatter, leveled achievements
- 5 save slots per profile; clearing the mountain deletes that slot, so every
  run is fresh
- The game asks before quitting

Controls
  Mouse        aim and throw candy
  Alt          toggle the cursor while playing
  Esc          save and return to the menu
  Space/Enter  skip cutscene
  M            open / close the Free Climb map
  Right click  teleport on the Free Climb map

Windows build, no installation needed. Settings live in
%APPDATA%\A Slipery Climb; each profile keeps its saves in the profiles
sub-folder.
